underway I heard many VHF messages calling vessels between Lanzarote and Morocco, in search for small inflatable refugee boats; very nice to hear that the RCC are taking actions and put in effort; apparently they dont't know the refugees exactly location, because the radius of called vessels is quite large and still far away from Lanzarote; my position is also far away; with little wind and at night I see almost no chance that I can help. 

approaching anchorage at darkness with 6 or 7 boats already at anchor; Christian gave me a great briefing about his and their positions, so it was easy for me to find my place in that crowd
