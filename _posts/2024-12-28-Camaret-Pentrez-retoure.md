Special trip with two purposes: 

1. to honour the winter solstice
1. to visit very special friends at Pentrez

Epropulsion engine worked on the first part of the trip, when I used it to compensate the very calm wind. Anchoring and lifting the anchor I did without trying engine support anyway. On the way back, the engine failed sporadically and more and more frequently, making it useless.

## Encounters

dolphins at night

## Incidents

engine failed when passing between the rocks of Pen Hir and for the harbour entry - passing Pen Hir under sail worked, but with limited maneouverability due to very calm wind; harbour entry worked with tacking

## Lessons learned

never depend on the engine (not a new lesson, just a confirmation)
