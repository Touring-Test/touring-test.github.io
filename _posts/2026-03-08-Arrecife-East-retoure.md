Motivation was part introduction and training for Clement, part SAR (yet not close enough to MA or not South enough to find vessels adrift)

Nice wind around force 6 from NNE, as expected. Windspeed slightly higher after the first 2 NM (sheltered by the island).

Waves were unexpectedly steep and choppy, causing a somewhat oncomfortable ride. Both sailors felt motion sickness(!)

We sailed with midsail in 2nd reef and aftsail in 1st reef from the beginning. Especially with the choppy waves this was a good, conservative choice for an easy ride.

Boran showed Clement how to boatsurf, which both enjoyed in long sessions.

Waves were pushing the rigid SUP against the deck from below. 1 fender between SUP and deck not sufficient - try 2 next time.

Difficult anchoring in the end, 3 attempts until anchor apparently held, yet it moved 7 meters during the night in relatively light wind. So we went to the inside anchor position next morning for better anchoring during the next week of stronger wind.
