Sail-and-Rescue patrol #3 with no sightings

Crossing the Strait of Gibraltar from La Línea de la Concepción to Ceuta and back without any sightings of vessels in distress. Weather conditions were moderate breeze, making for a comfortable crossing.

The Strait's current is nicely visible in the GPS track, causing a lead angle of 30° or more.

Excellent view of the Moroccan mountains behind Ceuta.

Today I can sail past Ceuta to the South side. Earlier I saw another sailboat leaving this area. Once arrived, I saw a service vessel (GV Adorne) in standby. After I tacked in the bay very slowly, then heave to for a pause, the service vessel approached and informed me that anchoring is not allowed here. Weird, because there is no corresponding entry in the sea charts. Looks like a temporary restriction.

Anyway, I sailed back. Underway hit a massive standing wave.

Most scenic sunset in the middle of the Strait.
