Efficient trip in strong winds, mostly from behind. Could see land most of the time.

I found out by experimenting that butterfly is possible but requires very precise steering on Maru. I changed strategy to gybe a bit before the wind.

Preparing the boat for harbour entry in Klintholm at strong, onshore winds challenged me. With a huge safety distance it was no problem after all.

## Incidents

One mooring line came loose and drifted in the water. Found out when I deployed the engine for harbour entry.
