I had been very nervous before this trip, for reasons unknown to me. 

Planned to visit Norderney, but then I had doubts whether I could safely approach the island through shallow water and potentially waves that build up high and break.

I changed the plan and continued sailing Southwest, which allowed me a approach with crosswind. Also the strong wind calmed down a bit by the time I reached Vlieland.

Effectively crossed the German Bight.
