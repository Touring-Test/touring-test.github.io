Special trip with Carole to dear friends.

Sailed without engine because the old one was defective and the new one has not arrived yet.

Challenging anchor approach 

## Incidents

1. During the approach to Pentrez, the wind picked up to a fresh breeze. It flipped the SUP around which had been inflated and strapped to the side of the starboard hull. Had to recover and deflate the SUP while sailing.

1. The turnbuckle of the starboard-rear shroud disconnected. Luckily no part was lost, and I could connect it while sailing.

## Lessons learned

1. Deflate and store SUP below deck while sailing.

1. Check all turnbuckles on all shrouds every time in the take-off procedure (it is part of the checklist)
