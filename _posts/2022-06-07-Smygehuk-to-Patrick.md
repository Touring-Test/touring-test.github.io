Surprisingly efficient trip around Malmö and through the Kattegat, with almost no tacking.

Passing below the bridge was quite scenic, and marine traffic in the Kattegat was not as bad as I expected.

Destination is Patrick's and Ebba's home.

Approaching the destination in a fresh breeze and after a night of sailing gave me some difficulties. I knew that it will be an anchor approach in onshore wind, and I had no prior experience with that. In particular, I thought that I need to drop the main sail for this manoeuver.

Upon my arrival, Patrick visited me on the boat by swimming.
